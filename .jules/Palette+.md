# Palette+ Design Decision Ledger

**Components Addressed:**
- **Buttons** (`.btn`, `.btn-primary`, `.btn-danger`, `.btn-success`, `.btn-icon`): Injected subtle hover scaling and smooth transitions. Replaced harsh interactions with elegant micro-interactions.
- **Inputs & Focus Rings** (`.form-control`): Added robust, branded `box-shadow` configurations for the `focus-visible` ring.
- **Empty States** (`.empty-state-container`, `.empty-state-icon`): Enriched presentation with increased padding, size, and gentle `fadeIn` and `slideInUp` animations to prevent empty data sets from looking broken.
